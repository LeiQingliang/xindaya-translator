# Community Code of Conduct · 社区行为准则

This project welcomes participation regardless of background, identity, experience, or preferred language. Keep discussion respectful, specific, and focused on improving the work.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas, translations, and code without attacking people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and remain open to correction.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and the right to decline a request.
- Make room for contributors who are new to the project or writing in another language.

Harassment, threats, hateful abuse, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and publishing someone's private information without permission are not acceptable. Spam and repeated disruption are also unwelcome.

## Scope and moderation

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other community spaces maintained for this repository. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation proportionately to the behavior. They should explain moderation decisions when doing so is appropriate and does not reveal private information.

If a conversation becomes unsafe, stop engaging and use GitHub's report or block controls. For project-specific concerns, ask a maintainer for a private channel without disclosing private incident details publicly. GitHub's own policies also apply to activity on GitHub.

## 中文说明

欢迎不同背景、经验和语言的贡献者。请讨论具体问题，尊重不同意见与个人隐私。可以指出代码、规则或译文的问题，不应攻击贡献者本人。

骚扰、威胁、歧视辱骂、未经允许公开他人信息、垃圾信息和持续干扰讨论均不可接受。维护者可按情节删除不当内容、关闭讨论或限制参与；在不泄露隐私的前提下，尽可能说明处理原因。

遇到不安全的交流时，可停止互动并使用 GitHub 的举报或屏蔽功能。涉及个人情况时，先请求私密沟通渠道，不在公开讨论中披露敏感细节。
